Snow Leopard

Panthera uncia

About the Snow Leopard

The snow leopard is a majestic big cat known for its stunning coat and elusive behavior. Native to the mountain ranges of Central and South Asia, it is well adapted to cold climates and rugged terrains.

Quick Facts

  • Habitat: Mountain ranges, especially the Himalayas.
  • Diet: Carnivorous, feeding on animals like sheep and goats.
  • Lifespan: 15-18 years in the wild.
  • Status: Vulnerable (IUCN Red List).
